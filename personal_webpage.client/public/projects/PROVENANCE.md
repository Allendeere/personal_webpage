# Asset provenance

All project images and the gameplay video were supplied by Sarah from her portfolio directory. Images are resized and encoded as WebP. At Sarah's request, the two company logos and their wordmarks were removed from the header regions of all eight corporate website screenshots on 2026-10-31 using imagegen edits composited into the original images. Other image content is preserved. Edit regions, patches, and the exact prompt are recorded in scripts/asset-edits/corporate/. Original filenames and dimensions are recorded in src/asset-manifest.json. The retired magnetic-game composite and duplicate speech-therapy image are excluded.
